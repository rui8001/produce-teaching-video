# Teaching-video production workflow

1. Lock the brief: learner, objective, platform, duration, style, source scope, model limits, transfer task, and authorization.
2. Build the source ledger and demonstration plan. Expose `needs_source` and `needs_asset` instead of fabricating completeness.
3. Lock the spoken script. Keep stage directions and visual actions elsewhere.
4. Create or import accepted continuous narration. For multiple roles, follow the dedicated audio reference.
5. Align captions from the real audio. Require a continuous, gap-free timeline ending at the accepted audio duration.
6. Plan semantic shots with viewer takeaway, visual role, initial state, meaningful changes, evidence, labels, transition, and accessibility notes.
7. Produce or select assets. Review contact sheets before opening only the originals that need close inspection.
8. Build a representative sample. Review subject accuracy, learning sequence, visual state, captions, transitions, audio, rights, and privacy.
9. After sample approval, complete the full timeline without silently changing locked teaching claims or the accepted voice.
10. Run final QC and retain the final video, final captions, source/QC report, and rollback information.

Feedback after publication should change one main variable at a time. Engagement metrics do not override subject accuracy or learning evidence.
